#  Career Breakthrough — Start Here

> Build a job-search loop you can actually maintain: evidence → applications → interviews → reviews → stronger evidence.

Career Breakthrough is a Markdown toolkit for students and early-career job seekers. Copy the templates into a **private local workspace** and make them your own. It is not a recruiting platform, an automated application service, or a promise of an offer.

## The 30-minute setup

### 1. Establish your starting point

Write down three things in a private note:

- **Target:** one role family and one time window, e.g. “embedded internship, next 8 weeks”.
- **Evidence:** two projects, classes, results, or experiences you can explain truthfully.
- **Gap:** one capability you expect to be questioned about.

Then read [01 · Mass Application Mindset](methodology/01-海投心法.md), [02 · The Funnel](methodology/02-筛选漏斗.md), and [03 · Prove Yourself](methodology/03-证明自己.md).

### 2. Turn experience into evidence

Choose a resume template from [`resume/templates/`](resume/templates/) and use the [STAR guide](resume/ai-generation-guide/STAR法则.md). Keep only claims you can explain, demonstrate, and defend in an interview.

**Output:** a first resume version such as `resume-v1` and a small evidence list for each project.

### 3. Create your private operating board

Copy [求职闭环看板](pipeline/求职闭环看板.md) and [企业追踪模板](pipeline/企业追踪模板.md) to a private workspace. Add the first 3–5 relevant opportunities.

For every opportunity, log the role, the key JD signal, the resume version you used, a next action, and a due date.

**Output:** a pipeline you can act on this week, rather than a folder of saved links.

### 4. Prepare and interview

Before an interview, use [面试准备清单](interview/面试准备清单.md). Practice with [AI 面试模拟](ai-tools/AI面试模拟.md) only on experiences that are real.

**Output:** three project stories, likely questions, and questions you want to ask the interviewer.

### 5. Review and write back

Within 24 hours, fill [面试复盘模板](interview/面试复盘模板.md). Extract one market signal and one action with a due date. Complete it, then update your resume, evidence list, or job-search strategy.

**Output:** the next iteration is visibly stronger than the last one.

## The weekly rhythm

- **Monday (10 min):** choose the 3–5 applications or follow-ups that matter most.
- **After each interview (15 min):** record questions, evidence gaps, and an action item.
- **Friday (20 min):** review reply rate, interview conversion, repeated questions, and overdue actions in the [board](pipeline/求职闭环看板.md#5-每周回顾20-分钟).

## Privacy first

Keep all real contact details, employer names, interview recordings, application screenshots, and private feedback outside this public repository. Use aliases in public discussions and contributions. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
